## Visual system

- Canvas: 1920x1080, 30fps, 60 seconds.
- Background: ink navy `#0b1220`, surfaces `#121c2c` and `#18263a`.
- Main accent: mint `#79e0c2`; support: warm paper `#f5f1e8`; warning/attention: coral `#ff967d`.
- Type: `Microsoft YaHei`, `Segoe UI`, `Arial`; metadata uses `Cascadia Mono`.
- Motion language: deliberate editorial scroll, mechanical panel assembly, restrained ambient drift.
